Privacy Policy

Swat Islamia Paper Maker (“the app”) is provided by Swat Islamia Educational Complex, Saidu Sharif, Swat.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Your papers

Papers, questions and settings that you create are saved only on your own device (in the app’s local storage). They are never sent to our servers or to any third party. Word files you export are created on your device and saved where you choose.

Children

The app is intended for teachers and school staff and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
